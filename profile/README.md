## Who We Are

SmartAvatar B.V. is a European deep-tech company focused on turning
advanced technology and research into practical, commercially viable
solutions. We work at the intersection of applied AI, connected systems,
secure digital platforms, and industry transformation.

Our work connects research, engineering, and real-world applications ---
helping organisations explore new capabilities and build solutions for
complex operational challenges.

## Working Together

We aim to keep our engineering work clear, maintainable, and secure.

For contributors, please follow the guidance in each repository:

1.  Read the project README before starting.
2.  Follow the documented development and branching workflow.
3.  Keep pull requests focused and provide context for changes.
4.  Never commit credentials, secrets, personal data, or production
    keys.
5.  Report security concerns privately using the contact route
    documented by the project.

Repository-specific contribution and security policies take precedence
where provided.
