## About SmartAvatar

SmartAvatar B.V. is a European deep-tech company focused on turning advanced technology and research into practical, commercially viable solutions.

We work at the intersection of applied AI, connected systems, secure digital platforms, and industry transformation. Our goal is to bridge the gap between advanced research and real-world applications.

## Our Technology Domains

We explore and develop solutions across a range of emerging technology domains.
